Toronto Schools Atlas

Browse TDSB and TCDSB elementary and secondary schools in Toronto. Markers are categorized by both board colour and school-level shape.

School list: Ontario Ministry of Education, August 2026. Locations: City of Toronto Open Data, May 2026. The school directory is a snapshot; school details may change.

Interactive map powered by MapLibre GL JS and OpenFreeMap. OpenStreetMap and map-provider attribution remains available on the map.
